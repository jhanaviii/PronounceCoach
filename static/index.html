      Privacy notice (DPDP Act 2023)

      What we collect: only the audio you submit, only with your consent, only to generate pronunciation feedback. Storage & retention: audio is held in server memory for the seconds it takes to analyze, then discarded — retention is zero; no database, no files, no accounts. Processing: audio is transcribed by a speech-recognition API and the transcript is reviewed by an AI model; these processors do not retain your data for training under their API terms. Your rights: close the tab or press "Try again" and every trace of your session is gone. Questions or grievances: contact the data fiduciary.
